Tucson Mural 1.0.0
First public release.

Ten original Tucson/Sonoran mural numerals with coordinated morning, day, sunset, and night lighting. Includes local date and weather, Fahrenheit/Celsius settings, optional Bluetooth disconnect icon and vibration, and optional Low Vision Mode with larger date/weather text.

Supported platforms: Emery (Pebble Time 2), Gabbro (Pebble Round 2), and Basalt (Pebble Time / Time Steel).

Same approved artwork and behavior as development version 0.11.0; release version metadata updated to 1.0.0. Existing installations retain settings and weather cache through the unchanged UUID.
